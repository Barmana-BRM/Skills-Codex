# Integrated Revision

Integrated revision prevents local edits from breaking the paper.

## Consistency Checks

- Construct names match across title, abstract, theory, hypotheses, measures, results, and discussion.
- Hypotheses and tables use the same direction and numbering.
- Sample definition is identical across data, methods, tables, appendices, and limitations.
- Claims in abstract and introduction are no stronger than the evidence in results.
- Mechanisms in theory are the same mechanisms tested in results.
- Figures and tables are cited in order.
- Limitations do not undermine the main identification without explanation.

## Revision Log

Record each major revision with: issue, decision, files affected, evidence affected, unresolved risk, next owner.
