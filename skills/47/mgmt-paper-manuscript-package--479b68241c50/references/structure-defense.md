# Structure Defense

Use this checklist before asking anyone to read the paper. The goal is to make the paper defensible before polishing.

## Ten Questions

1. What is the central research question in one sentence?
2. Why does the question matter to management theory rather than only to a local empirical setting?
3. What is the theoretical tension, contradiction, or missing mechanism?
4. Which audience should care first: organization, strategy, IS, marketing, operations, finance, or entrepreneurship?
5. What is the paper's cleanest contribution claim?
6. What would a skeptical reviewer say is not novel?
7. What is the strongest alternative explanation?
8. What evidence directly addresses mechanism rather than only association?
9. What is the boundary condition of the claim?
10. What exactly should managers learn that they could not infer from common sense?

## Output

Create a table with columns: question, current answer, weakness, needed evidence, owner, deadline.
