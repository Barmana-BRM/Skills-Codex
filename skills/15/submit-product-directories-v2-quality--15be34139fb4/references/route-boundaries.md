# Route boundaries

## Supported by SPD

- `directory listing`
- `request app`
- `claim listing`
- `account/profile` when the public profile is a governed product-discovery surface

## Separate workflows required

Do not execute articles, guest posts, press releases, editorial pitches, community/forum/social posts, cold email, mass contact-form outreach, resource-page or partnership outreach, reciprocal-link outreach, or paid-link acquisition as directory-form work.

Record the candidate as route-excluded and hand it to a workflow with its own content, communication, publication, consent, and anti-spam controls. Do not reuse SPD authorization for those routes.
