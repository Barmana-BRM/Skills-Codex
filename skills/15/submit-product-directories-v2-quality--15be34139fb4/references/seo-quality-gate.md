# SEO purpose and directory quality gate

## Hard purpose boundary

Use this workflow for product discovery, accurate public product profiles, qualified referral traffic, and durable directory presence. Stop if the primary purpose is ranking manipulation, PageRank transfer, dofollow acquisition, anchor-text scaling, or backlink-volume growth.

Google identifies automated link creation, low-quality directory links, excessive exchanges, and paid links that pass ranking credit as link spam. Paid or incentivized placement is acceptable only when the resulting link is appropriately qualified, such as with `rel="sponsored"` or `rel="nofollow"`.

## Mandatory per-site checks

Record each item before form work:

1. `Audience relevance`: `pass`, `fail`, or `unknown`.
2. `User/discovery value`: `pass`, `fail`, or `unknown`.
3. `Editorial/platform governance`: `pass`, `governed platform`, `fail`, or `unknown`.
4. `Paid/ranking-link offer`: `none`, `qualified paid placement`, `unqualified paid/ranking link`, or `unknown`.
5. `Directory/network quality`: `pass`, `fail`, or `unknown`.
6. `Automation/terms compatibility`: `allowed`, `manual-only`, `prohibited`, or `unknown`.
7. `Reciprocal link requirement`: `none`, `optional`, or `required`.

Set `Quality gate: passed` only when relevance and discovery value pass; governance is `pass` or `governed platform`; paid/ranking-link offer is `none` or `qualified paid placement`; network quality passes; reciprocal linking is not required; and the selected execution mode conforms to the site's terms.

Set `ineligible` with structured evidence for irrelevant sites, no genuine discovery value, unqualified paid/ranking links, forced reciprocal links, low-quality/general-submit networks, empty or templated listing pages, or prohibited automation. Do not use DA, DR, dofollow availability, backlink count, or a paid “ranking package” as a positive selection signal.

## Link recording

After publication, record the actual anchor, href, `rel`, commercial relationship, and check time. Use brand/product/naked-URL anchors by default. Reject repeated commercial exact-match anchors. Require `sponsored` or `nofollow` when payment, a gift, a discount, or another incentive influenced placement.
