# Crystalize Style Board

## Core Identity

Crystalize is an artistic medium, not a visual filter.

Target feeling: - translucent - warm - delicate - luminous - refined -
organic - quietly magical - editorial - minimalist

Avoid cold CGI, hard glass, jewelry-ad aesthetics, low-poly rendering,
shattered crystal, and excessive fantasy effects.

## Transformation

**Extract → Simplify → Recompose → Crystallize**

Preserve visual identity, recognizable subjects, characteristic colors,
important silhouettes, and atmospheric mood. Discard clutter, repetitive
details, photographic noise, insignificant objects, and unnecessary
background information.

## Composition

The final work should resemble an editorial illustration, art print,
postcard, zine, minimalist travel poster, or collectible artwork. Use
strong negative space and one clear focal point.

## Crystalline Material

Translucent, softly faceted, optically illuminated, warm,
color-preserving, organic.

### Facets

Default: **medium-low density**. Use irregular, elegant, varied,
moderately sized, structurally meaningful facets. Avoid uniform
triangles, hard diamonds, giant geometric cuts, shattered glass, and
mechanical low-poly geometry.

### Transparency

Local rather than global. Thin/backlit areas can be more transparent;
thick/shadowed areas retain stronger color.

### Original Color

Hard constraint. Change light behavior, not the fundamental color
identity.

### Optical Light

Soft directional light, internal illumination, subtle refraction,
localized highlights, transmitted light, restrained dispersion. Avoid
neon, bloom, magical aura, excessive sparkle, and constant rainbow
effects.

### Shadows

Soft, translucent, atmospheric, physically suggestive. Transmitted
shadows may be dispersed, refracted, subtly colored, and lighter than
opaque shadows.

## Signature Edge

The edge must not look like a cutout.

Use an **organic crystalline silhouette**: - fine - delicate -
irregular - translucent - softly refractive - naturally faceted -
intentionally varied

Allow small crystalline fragments, narrow extensions, subtle
protrusions, soft optical transitions, and occasional environmental
elements crossing the edge.

Do not use rigid polygon masks, smooth vector stickers, noisy erosion,
shattered edges, or uniform crystal spikes.

## Background

Possible directions: - ivory / warm white / cream / pale gray - deep
navy / blue-black / charcoal / deep plum / deep forest - source-derived
atmospheric gradients - subtle paper texture

Choose for harmonious material contrast. Keep the background quieter
than the subject.

## 2D First

The artwork should read as illustration before material simulation. Use
simplified shapes, intentional flat areas, painterly transitions,
controlled facets, optical light, and negative space.

## Target Feeling

> A quiet crystalline world reconstructed from a photograph.

Not a crystal filter. Not a 3D gemstone render. Not shattered glass.
