# Style Dimensions

Use these dimensions to compare a manuscript with target journal examples.

## Macro Structure

- Where the phenomenon appears.
- How quickly the research question appears.
- How contribution is staged.
- Whether theory precedes method clearly.

## Paragraph Function

- Opening tension.
- Debate positioning.
- Construct definition.
- Mechanism explanation.
- Hypothesis derivation.
- Evidence interpretation.
- Boundary and limitation.

## Sentence-Level Style

- Claim density.
- Transition clarity.
- Use of cautious verbs.
- Avoidance of unsupported superlatives.
- Balance between theoretical terms and concrete evidence.

## Formatting

- Heading depth.
- Table and figure references.
- Footnote discipline.
- Supplement references.
- Anonymous manuscript requirements.
