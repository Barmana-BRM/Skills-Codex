# Journal Review Profiles

These are stable taste profiles, not official policy claims. Use the journal's official author guidelines for current formatting and submission rules.

## AMJ

Strong preference for clear management phenomenon, theory contribution, mechanism, boundary conditions, and compelling introduction.

## ASQ

Values deep organizational theory, thick explanation, interpretive care, and strong connection between evidence and theoretical insight.

## SMJ

Values strategy contribution, competitive advantage logic, identification, mechanisms, and implications for firms and managers.

## Organization Science

Values theoretical mechanism, methodological credibility, interdisciplinary relevance, and organizational insight.

## MISQ

Values information systems contribution, technology-organization interaction, rigorous method, and theory-relevant evidence.

## Management Science

Values analytical or empirical rigor, precise claims, robust evidence, and relevance beyond a narrow setting.
