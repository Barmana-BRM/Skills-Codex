# Reviewer Roles

## Handling Editor

Checks journal fit, contribution, clarity, ethical boundaries, and whether the manuscript is worth scarce reviewer time.

## Theory Reviewer

Checks construct clarity, theoretical mechanism, contribution to conversation, boundary conditions, and whether hypotheses follow from theory.

## Methods Reviewer

Checks design credibility, identification, measurement, sampling, missing data, robustness, and alternative explanations.

## Empirical Reviewer

Checks tables, model specification, statistical interpretation, figure clarity, reproducibility, and whether results support claims.

## Qualitative Reviewer

Checks case selection, data access, coding process, evidence chain, representative quotes, and whether interpretation is grounded.

## Managerial Relevance Reviewer

Checks whether implications are specific, bounded, actionable, and linked to evidence rather than generic managerial advice.

## Skeptical Reviewer

Assumes the contribution is overstated until proven otherwise. Useful for pre-submission stress testing.
